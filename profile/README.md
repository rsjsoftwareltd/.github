# RSJ Software

Self-hosted IT tools for schools, built in the UK. Everything runs on your own Windows server, and your data stays on your network.

## Our products

| Product | What it does |
|---|---|
| [**Groundskeeper**](https://groundskeeper.rsjsoftware.com) | IT monitoring made for schools and trusts: one plain-English feed of what needs attention, with a free Community edition. [Wiki](https://github.com/rsjsoftwareltd/groundskeeper/wiki) and [releases](https://github.com/rsjsoftwareltd/groundskeeper/releases). |
| [**Courier**](https://courier.rsjsoftware.com) | Free, self-hosted notices to the Windows computers in a school, with a report of how far each one got. [Wiki](https://github.com/rsjsoftwareltd/courier/wiki) and [releases](https://github.com/rsjsoftwareltd/courier-beta/releases). |
| [**Caretaker**](https://caretaker.rsjsoftware.com) | Remote assistance for school technicians. |
| [**RSJ Agent**](https://agent.rsjsoftware.com) | The small agent our products run on, on your Windows computers and servers. |
